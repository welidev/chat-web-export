## What it does and does not do

- It runs inside the WhatsApp Web tab in your browser and reads only the chats the signed-in
  account can already see there.
- It does not bypass sign-in, end-to-end encryption, or any access control, and it cannot reach
  any other account.
- It does not send data anywhere. It saves files to your computer and nothing else.
- It uses no official API. WhatsApp may change WhatsApp Web at any time, and the tool may stop
  working without notice.
